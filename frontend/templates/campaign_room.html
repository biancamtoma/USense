ML Ad-Retention Model Explanation

Adaptive 70/30 Relevance & Attention Optimization

Campaign recommendations are ranked using a 70% Brief Fit / 30% Ad-Retention Probability blend. Brief Fit ensures cognitive music-brand congruence (Heckler & Childers, 1992) so the music supports your message, while the Logistic Regression model predicts viewer skip likelihood based on historical attention telemetry.
